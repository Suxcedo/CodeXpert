How it works

Pick a language. Show up daily. Level up.

Three steps, repeated until it stops feeling like effort.

  1. 01

    Choose your language

    Python, JavaScript, Java, C++, HTML, CSS or C#. Start with one — switch whenever you like.

  2. 02

    Do one short lesson

    Five to ten minutes. Lessons unlock in order, so you always know exactly where you stand.

  3. 03

    Keep the streak alive

    Earn XP, badges and a spot on your group leaderboard. Miss a day and the streak resets.

Questions

Everything you might be wondering.

Is CodeXpert really free to start?

Yes. You can create an account, pick a language and complete the full learning path without paying. Streak rewards and prizes are open to everyone on the free plan.

How much time do I need each day?

Five to ten minutes is enough to keep a streak alive. Lessons are deliberately short so that a busy day never becomes an excuse to skip.

What happens if I miss a day?

Your streak resets to zero. Your XP, badges and completed lessons stay exactly where they are — you just start a new streak the next day.

Which languages can I learn?

Python, JavaScript, Java, C++, HTML, CSS and C#. You can switch languages at any time, and your progress in each is tracked separately.

Do I need any experience to begin?

None at all. The learning path starts from absolute zero and unlocks one lesson at a time, so you are never dropped into something you are not ready for.
